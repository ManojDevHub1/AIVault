About Speechify

Accessibility-first text-to-speech reader that reads articles, textbooks, PDFs, and emails out loud at customizable speeds with natural voices.

Key Features

  • High-speed reading up to 4.5x without pitch distortion
  • Celebrity and natural voice options (including Snoop Dogg and Gwyneth Paltrow)
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) converting physical book photos to speech
  • Browser extension reading web pages and Google Docs aloud
  • Sync listening progress across iOS, Android, Mac, and Chrome
Best Suited For
People with ADHD or dyslexia, Busy professionals, Students reading heavy literature
